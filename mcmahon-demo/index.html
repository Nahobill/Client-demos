Our Practice

A private practice built on 37 years of trust

Nana Girgis McMahon, MD has maintained a private pediatric practice at Emerson Hospital in Concord, MA since 1987. Dr. McMahon and her staff are committed to giving the highest quality medical care to infants, children, adolescents, and college-age patients in a compassionate, family-friendly environment.

The practice provides organized, evidence-based care — coordinating with specialists when needed and reminding families proactively about needed tests and exams. Care is seamless, built around standard protocols, and always personalized.

🏆 2024 Community Physician of the Year — Dr. McMahon has been awarded this honor by the Middlesex Central District Medical Society, recognizing her decades of exceptional service to the Concord community.

Dr. McMahon was also recognized by NCQA for systematic use of patient-centered, coordinated care management processes.

New Patients

Joining our practice

We look forward to welcoming your family. Here's what to know before your first visit — we've made it as straightforward as possible.

1

Notify your insurance

Let your insurance provider know that Dr. McMahon has been selected as your child's primary care provider.

2

Gather records

Request copies of all medical records and complete immunization history from birth from prior physicians.

3

Complete registration forms

Give us a call and we'll mail or have registration forms ready for you, or simply fill them out in person when you arrive for your first visit.

4

Bring to your first visit

Insurance card, photo ID, completed registration forms, immunization records, and copayment.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. McMahon accepting new patients?
Yes. Dr. Nana G. McMahon is currently accepting new pediatric patients, from newborns through college age.
How do I schedule an appointment?
All appointments are scheduled by phone at (978) 369-5050. The practice does not offer online self-scheduling.
What are your office and phone hours?
The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:00am to 5:00pm. Phone lines are staffed Monday through Friday, 7:30am to 6:00pm. Outside of those hours, a 24/7 on-call answering service is available for urgent care.
Do you offer same-day sick visits?
Yes, same-day sick visit appointments are reserved daily for urgent needs.
What hospital is the practice affiliated with?
Dr. McMahon has been affiliated with Emerson Hospital in Concord, MA since 1987.
What languages does Dr. McMahon speak?
Dr. McMahon speaks English, French, and Arabic.
What should I bring to my child's first visit?
Please bring an insurance card, photo ID, completed registration forms, immunization records, and any applicable copayment. Registration forms are available in the Forms section of this site.
